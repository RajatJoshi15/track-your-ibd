Track Your IBD

Privacy

Last updated 27 September 2026

The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your own device.
  • There's no account and no sign-up, and we never see your data.
  • No analytics, ads, trackers or cookies.
  • Backups go only where you choose to save them.

What the app stores

Track Your IBD saves what you type into it: bathroom trips, symptoms and check-ins, meals and food ratings, water, sleep, naps, exercise, medicines, vitamins and supplements, doses, periods and cycle symptoms (only if you turn on period tracking), notes and your settings. It keeps them in your web browser's storage on the device you're using. They're never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Each device keeps its own separate log. Your phone and your computer don't share data unless you move a backup from one to the other.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no cookies. Once it has loaded, it doesn't send anything over the internet. It works offline.

Website hosting

The app's files are hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify may keep basic technical records when your browser downloads those files, such as your IP address, browser type and the time. This is for security and reliability. Those records never include anything you enter in the app. See Netlify's privacy policy.

Backups and exports

When you save a backup or a spreadsheet, you choose where it goes, for example Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Files, email or your Downloads folder. That copy is then looked after by the service you chose, under its own terms. Backup files aren't encrypted, so keep them somewhere private and only share them with people you trust, such as your doctor.

Your control

  • You can change or delete any entry at any time.
  • More › Delete all data erases everything the app stored on that device.
  • Clearing your browser's site data, or removing the installed app, also deletes it.
  • We don't keep a copy. If you lose your device or clear its data without a backup, your entries can't be recovered.

Not medical advice

Track Your IBD is a personal record-keeping tool. Period predictions are rough estimates and must not be used for contraception. The activity scores are estimates of published symptom indexes: the Simple Clinical Colitis Activity Index for ulcerative colitis and the Harvey-Bradshaw Index for Crohn's disease. They are worked out from what you log. They don't diagnose, treat or replace care from your doctor. Don't change your treatment based on the app. Get urgent medical help for heavy bleeding, severe belly pain, a high fever or signs of dehydration.

Children

The app isn't aimed at children. A parent or carer may use it to keep a record for a child in their care.

Changes to this page

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Because we don't collect your data, a change can't affect what we already hold about you. We hold nothing.
