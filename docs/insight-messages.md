# Plain-language insight messages (not built yet)

Saved 28 September 2026 for a later version. The idea: lead with sentences people understand, and keep the numbers and charts one tap away. Words in {braces} are filled in from each person's data.

Planned placement: insights and the weekly summary at the top of **Trends**; early warnings and good streaks on **Today**. Each message should only appear when the data supports it (see "How sure the app is").

## Food and drinks

- **Worse:** "Your symptoms tend to be worse the day after **{food}**. It happened {k} of the last {n} times."
- **Better:** "The day after **{food}** was usually calmer for you ({k} of the last {n} times)."
- **Worse:** "Meals high in **fat** were usually followed by a rougher day."
- **Better:** "Days with more **fibre** were usually followed by a calmer day."
- **Worse:** "After days with **alcohol**, your next day was usually rougher."
- **Worse:** "Days you **smoked** were often followed by a rougher day."
- **No link:** "So far, **{food}** doesn't show a clear link with how you feel the next day."

For alcohol and smoking, only use the "worse" and "no link" versions, so the app never seems to recommend them.

## Meal timing

- **Worse:** "When you went **5+ hours without eating**, the next day was often rougher."
- **Better:** "**Regular meals** with no long gaps were usually followed by calmer days."
- **Worse:** "**Eating close to bedtime** was often followed by a rougher day."
- **Better:** "**Finishing dinner earlier** seems to suit you. Those next days were usually calmer."
- **Fact:** "About **{x} in 10** of your daytime bathroom trips come within an hour of eating. That's common with IBD and worth mentioning to your doctor."

## Medicines

- **Worse:** "After **missed doses**, your next day was usually rougher."
- **Better:** "Days after taking **all your doses** were usually calmer."
- **Worse:** "Doses taken **2+ hours late** were often followed by a rougher day."
- **Worse:** "When **{medicine}** was taken away from food, the next day was often rougher."
- **Positive:** "You took **every dose** this week. Nice work."

## Sleep

- **Worse:** "After nights **under 6 hours**, your next day was usually rougher."
- **Better:** "After a **full night's sleep** (7+ hours), your next day was usually calmer."
- **Worse:** "Going to bed **after midnight** was often followed by a rougher day."
- **Better:** "Sticking to your **usual bedtime** seems to help."
- **Worse:** "Nights you **woke from pain** were usually followed by a harder day."

## Periods (if tracked)

- **Worse:** "Your symptoms tend to be **higher on period days**."
- **No link:** "Your periods don't seem to change your IBD symptoms so far."

## Weekly summary

- **Better week:** "**Better than last week.** Fewer bathroom trips ({x} a day, down from {y}), no blood since {day}, and you took all your doses."
- **Tougher week:** "**A tougher week than last.** Bathroom trips went up to {x} a day (from {y}), and there was blood on {n} days. Keep logging, and consider contacting your IBD team if it continues."
- **Steady:** "**Much the same as last week.** About {x} trips a day and {h} hours of sleep a night."

## Today: early warnings and good streaks

- **Gentle warning:** "Your bathroom trips have gone up **3 days in a row**. Keep an eye on it."
- **Stronger warning:** "Your symptoms have been rising for **{n} days**, and you've seen blood on **{k} days** this week. If it keeps up, it may be worth contacting your IBD team."
- **Good streak:** "**{n} calm days in a row.** Your longest stretch this month."
- **Good streak:** "**No blood for {n} days.**"
- **Good streak:** "Your symptoms have **eased over the last week**."

## How sure the app is

Put one label in front of each insight:

- **"Early hint:"** it has only happened 3–4 times so far
- **"Likely pattern:"** 5–9 times, with a clear difference
- **"Strong pattern:"** 10+ times, with a big difference

**Not enough data yet:** "Keep logging for about **{n} more days** to see what affects your symptoms."

**Footer on every insight card:** "These are patterns in your own log, not proof. Talk to your doctor before changing your diet or medicines."
